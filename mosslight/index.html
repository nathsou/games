THE CAPTAIN’S FIELD GUIDE

Leave no leaf behind.

Bring all three golden lanterns to the ship. Send enough crew to a task, then let them work while you explore. Carriers find their own way home.

Move
WASD / arrows, or click empty ground. Hold Shift while moving to sprint. Click the map to travel farther.
Send crew
Click a berry, lantern, enemy, or bridge to send your selected crew. E sends crew to the nearest task. Space sends them toward your pointer.
Throw one
Shift + click sends a single friend. Useful for splitting jobs.
Whistle
Right click to call friends near the pointer. Hold Q to call friends around you. Calling workers takes them off their task.
Select
1 Ember · 2 Tide · 3 Honey · 4 Everyone. Or use the crew buttons.
Rest
Esc / P pauses. M toggles sound. The game pauses when you switch tabs.

Look for glowing sprouts. Walk close to recruit them. Honey friends count as two carriers. Tide friends can cross the stream; everyone else needs the bridge or the southern ford. If a friend wilts in a fight, whistle them awake before their glow fades. Your crew can never be permanently wiped out: the ship grows reinforcements when only a few remain.

Try “Take your time” for a relaxed expedition with no deadline.
