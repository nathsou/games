# Original Yesteryear artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 9 October 2026, from text prompts alone. No Cluance images, commercial game artwork or reference images were supplied or adapted. The illustrations are original stylized interpretations of historical events, objects and cultural subjects, not documentary reconstructions. Some pop-culture cards use symbolic still lifes rather than characters.

All 305 cards have an individual illustration, mapped by stable card ID in `src/artwork.js`. Thirteen 6 × 4 atlases hold up to 24 illustrations each; the final seven cells of `events-13` are unused. English and French titles use the same art. Titles and years remain accessible HTML, and years are absent from artwork filenames and atlas metadata.

Production WebPs are 1200 × 800 at quality 92, using nearest-neighbor resizing. The browser loads only atlases needed by the currently rendered cards. Original PNG outputs remain outside Git. `events-08` was regenerated because the first output depicted the flying shuttle as a spacecraft and used a portrait grid; the accepted prompt names the weaving tool explicitly and specifies a landscape image.
