# French Departments

Separate from French Regions, which keeps the 27 administrative regions of 2015. This deck uses all **101 department names and codes** in the French government's [geography API](https://geo.api.gouv.fr/departements?fields=nom,code,codeRegion), retrieved on 4 October 2026: 96 metropolitan departments (including Corse-du-Sud 2A and Haute-Corse 2B) and five overseas departments (971, 972, 973, 974 and 976). It does not add Saint-Martin, Saint-Barthélemy, overseas collectivities or the Lyon metropolis as departments.

Cards have stable official-code IDs, individual local landscapes, a code/landmark subtitle and contextual biography. Illustrations are symbolic local scenes, not boundary maps; no map accuracy is claimed. Rhône uses Villefranche-sur-Saône and Beaujolais, rather than presenting Lyon as part of the current department. Generated illustrations remain stylized interpretations of architecture.

Artwork was generated and individually reviewed in both game and AI crops. Only compressed WebP delivery assets are committed. Each standard atlas uses a 5 × 2 grid; Mayotte and the final Saint-Denis/Limoges replacements are single portraits; other corrections use 3 × 2 or 3 × 1 grids. Full prompts, output identifiers and any correction mappings are recorded in `assets/DEPARTMENT_PROMPTS.md`.

## Validation

- Official names/codes match all 101 government API entries exactly; 96 metropolitan and five overseas subjects appear once. Stable IDs are `departments-<official code>`. Regions contains exactly its 27 administrative subjects.
- All 101 cards individually inspected in both real production layouts. Eleven initial cells were replaced for misleading architecture or a clipped prop. Every department searched/opened with matching biography and date caption; both collection sorts, 1440/390/320px galleries and the phone inspector pass. All 541 active and 27 archived records render, with distinct images within each theme in both layouts and no browser or failed asset requests.
- 1,152 complete five-round engine games cover all twelve themes as board/clue decks, both Classic/Fixed variants and four seed passes. Every department occurred both on a board and in a hand. Overlapping subjects stay out of clue pools; refills, saved projections and private-field exclusion pass. Retained card records remain unchanged.
- Complete Classic and Fixed browser games used a Departments board (including 2A/2B and all five overseas departments) with Regions clues. Guesser privacy, reload/resume after a clue, exported/reopened replays and all five replay steps passed. Archived region and province replays remain readable.
- Eight complete server Take turns games exercised Departments as board or clues, both creator roles and both variants, with private guesser information excluded. Static-site assembly, delivered-artwork presence, module syntax and whitespace checks pass. No paid AI calls or live cross-network multiplayer were needed for these deck changes.

The fifteen compressed department assets total **7,252,344 bytes (6.92 MiB)**. They were encoded directly from original imagegen PNGs at WebP quality 80/method 6; original dimensions and crop geometry are preserved. Alpes-Maritimes uses the caption “Re-established: 1860” to distinguish the modern department from its first creation in 1793.
