# French Departments

Separate from French Regions, which keeps the 27 administrative regions of 2015. This deck uses all **101 department names and codes** in the French government's [geography API](https://geo.api.gouv.fr/departements?fields=nom,code,codeRegion), retrieved on 4 October 2026: 96 metropolitan departments (including Corse-du-Sud 2A and Haute-Corse 2B) and five overseas departments (971, 972, 973, 974 and 976). It does not add Saint-Martin, Saint-Barthélemy, overseas collectivities or the Lyon metropolis as departments.

Cards have stable official-code IDs, individual local landscapes, a code/landmark subtitle and contextual biography. Illustrations are symbolic local scenes, not boundary maps; no map accuracy is claimed. Rhône uses Villefranche-sur-Saône and Beaujolais, rather than presenting Lyon as part of the current department. Generated illustrations remain stylized interpretations of architecture.

Artwork is being generated and individually reviewed in both game and AI crops. Only compressed WebP delivery assets are committed. Each standard atlas uses a 5 × 2 grid; Mayotte is a single separate portrait. Full prompts, output identifiers and any correction mappings will be recorded in `assets/DEPARTMENT_PROMPTS.md`.
