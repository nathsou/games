# Collection expansion and individual card audit

Work in progress: expand the ten 24-card themes to 30 illustrated subjects each, matching the publisher's [Similo: History deck description](https://horribleguild.com/eu/product/similo-history/): “Each version of Similo comes with a deck of 30 big cards”. French Regions is the complete 27-region administrative set of 2015; its final scope is pending the user's preference.

Required additions: Charles Babbage in Scientists, Jean-Paul Sartre in Philosophers, Albert Camus in Writers.

Review criteria for every active card: subject/roster match, recognisable silhouette, useful distinguishing attributes, anatomy/objects, period and geography, crop at the actual game ratio, text and date legibility, metadata consistency, and unintended duplicates. Artwork is illustrative; multi-landmark place scenes are symbolic montages, not literal geographic viewpoints.

Initial redesign findings: Homer has modern glasses; Diogenes has a wooden barrel instead of the traditional ceramic pithos; Clovis uses later medieval royal imagery; Mansa Musa uses a European crown; Ibn al-Haytham's prism conflates his optical work with Newton's spectrum; Marie Curie's fluorescent green liquid presents a misleading radiation cue. These subjects will receive completely new image-generated compositions.

Original card IDs and original atlas geometry remain stable. New subjects are appended and use supplementary atlases. Replacement art changes only the affected subjects' artwork mapping.

The final per-card review and browser verification will be recorded here as the work completes.
